# Beach — the scene (what the child sees behind the stickers)

Written 2026-10-10 from the pack's art at v6 (`packs/beach/art/background*.webp`
and `foreground*.webp`, viewed whole and with stickers pasted at size).
Positions are fractions of the base 4:3 picture (what an iPad shows),
measured from the **left** and from the **top**; the wide picture (phones)
adds more sea, sand and sky at both sides. Wide screens crop a band off
the top and the bottom (and the tray covers the bottom one).

A sunny, sheltered sandy cove in daylight, painted in the same soft
picture-book gouache as Forest.

## What is not there

No pier, jetty, harbour or boats painted (the boats are stickers); no
island, no cave, no cliff to climb near us, no dunes, no beach huts,
no trees, palms or grass on the beach; no path, steps, road or town; no
people painted; no lifeguard tower; no sun, moon, stars or birds painted
(the effects and the stickers bring them); no tide-line marks, no
puddles. Nothing changes the picture's water level: never say the tide
came in over the sand or the sea covered the castle on screen.

## What the canvas effects can and cannot change

Allowed (outdoors): `sunshine`, `clouds`, `wind`, `rain`, `rainbow`,
`fog`, `sunset`, `night`, `shootingstars`, `confetti`, `bubbles`. Not
used here: `snow` (not a beach day), `leaves` (no trees), `fireflies`
(not a beach thing).

- `sunshine`: warm light over everything, a bright sunny day.
- `clouds`: soft cloud shadows drift over; the painted clouds stay.
- `wind`: blows left to right; anything carried by it goes right.
- `rain` / `rainbow`: rain falls over the scene, a rainbow arcs after.
  The sand stays golden: no puddles, no mud.
- `fog`: a sea mist over the scene; the lighthouse fades but stays painted.
- `sunset` / `night`: golden evening light, then a dark blue night with
  a moon. The lighthouse is painted unlit: say "the lighthouse kept watch",
  not "its beam swept the sea" (no beam is drawn).
- `shootingstars`: over night only.
- `confetti`, `bubbles`: a party, bubbles rising (a pool party, a bubbly
  fish).

**Kids at night:** Lola and Nico never appear in a story that reaches
`night` or `shootingstars` (the user's rule, 2026-10-10).
