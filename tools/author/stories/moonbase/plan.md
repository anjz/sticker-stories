# Moon Base — story roster (v2, the redone cast)

**Audience: 8–10** (GUIDE "Audience by pack"). 80–140 words a language,
every safety rule as for four-year-olds, but a real plot with a twist,
missions, problem-solving, dry humour and one true piece of Moon science
where a row carries `learning`. Realistic space only: no aliens or
creatures, and the machines have no faces — they show what they feel in
what they do (their actions), and the narrator may wonder about them.
The scene is `scene.md`; the canvas effects are the space setting's
(`comet`, `nightfall`, `daybreak`, `eclipse`, `milkyway`, `satellites`,
`dust`, `floodlights`, `glints`). Narration is Eleven v4, one take; the
two astronauts speak their own lines (`character-voices.json`).

**Particles.** Landings, lift-offs, the lope, the drill, the dig and the
buggy's wheelspin puff dust on their own frames: the words name the dust
without a cue. `dust-puff`, `spray` and `sparks` are for dust, water or
sparks the words make that no animation draws (a stamp, a skid, a tool).
Moon dust drifts down slowly and hangs.

## Cast (what each sticker's art shows)

The two astronauts are **Maya** (the woman) and **Leo** (the man): the
base's whole crew. Everyone else is a machine, a plant or a piece of the
sky — *it*.
