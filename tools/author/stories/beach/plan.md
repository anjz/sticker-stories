# Beach — story roster

**Audience: 4–8** (picture-book, like Forest). 80–140 words a language,
every safety rule. The scene is `scene.md`; canvas effects are the
outdoors setting's minus `snow`, `leaves` and `fireflies`. Narration is
Eleven v4, one take; Lola (the girl) and Nico (the surfer) speak their
own lines (`character-voices.json`).

**Pack rules (the user's):** no story that reaches `night` or
`shootingstars` has Lola or Nico in it, and they never get a sleeping
face. Nico out at sea (`{surfer:go to sea by surf}`) stays on his board:
no action or face on him until he rides back in
(`{surfer:go to sand by surf}`). Boats, the ring and the bottle float on
the sea and come in from the open water on the left. The creatures' home
is the rock pool (`pool`) among the rocks (`rocks`); the little pool at
our feet is scenery, never a place.
