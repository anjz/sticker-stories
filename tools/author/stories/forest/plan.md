# Forest — story roster (v2)

50 stories: 4 fallbacks (no featured stickers) + 46 centred on 3–4 stickers.
Every sticker is featured at least 5 times and is the hero of at least one
story. Setting `outdoors`: canvas effects allowed are `fog`, `rain`,
`sunshine`, `rainbow`, `night`; used in 20 stories (40 %). 21 stories (42 %)
carry one small true fact.
