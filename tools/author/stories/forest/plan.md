# Forest — story roster (v5)

2026-09-26: the whole set rewritten from scratch (nothing kept from v4).
50 stories: 4 fallbacks (no featured stickers) + 46 centred on 3–4 stickers.
Every sticker is featured at least 5 times and is the hero of at least one
story. Setting `outdoors`: canvas effects in 34 stories, wherever the words
describe weather or light, using all 13 allowed effects. 22 stories (44 %)
carry one small true fact. Every sticker's action plays in at least three
stories; about half the stories hold one on its pause pose.

Writing rule new in this set (GUIDE "Live stickers"): **name a live action
in a few words** ("Badger dug a hole") and let the story move on; describe
its frames only when the story uses the detail later.

counts or describes a visible feature uses these, not what the real animal
