# Forest — story roster (v3)

50 stories: 4 fallbacks (no featured stickers) + 46 centred on 3 stickers.
Every sticker is featured at least 5 times and is the hero of at least one
story. Setting `outdoors`: canvas effects in 35 stories (70 %), wherever the words
describe weather or light, using all 13 allowed effects. 19 stories (38 %) carry one small true fact. Duckling replaced Tree on 2026-09-24 (the painted trees are scenery and a stage feature now).
v4 (2026-09-25): every sticker has a live action — most with a pause pose
a story can hold (`{snail:live hold}` … `{snail:live resume}`) — and a move
its entrance plays (walk, hop, crawl, roll, flight, sprout). The stickers
no longer carry lily pads, twigs or leaves. Stories play two to four
actions each, on the words that tell them, and about half hold one; every
action plays in at least four stories.
