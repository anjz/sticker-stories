# Dinosaurs — the scene (what the child sees behind the stickers)

Written 2026-10-30 from the pack's art (`packs/dinosaurs/art/sky*.webp`,
`background*.webp`, `layer-treefern*.webp`, `layer-araucaria*.webp`,
`foreground*.webp`), viewed whole with a grid drawn over them. Positions
are fractions of the base 4:3 picture (what an iPad shows), measured from
the **left** and from the **top**; the wide picture (phones) adds more
lagoon and the tree fern's trunk on the left, more meadow and the
monkey-puzzle's trunk on the right. Wide screens crop a band off the top
and the bottom.

A sunny prehistoric valley in daylight, painted in the same soft
picture-book style as Forest: a lagoon, a fern meadow, a big flat rock,
two strange old trees, and far away flat-topped red cliffs and one
sleepy old mountain.

## What is not there

No river, stream, waterfall or path; no cave, burrow, nest or hut painted
(the nest is the `eggs` sticker, the hollow log is the `log` sticker, the
mud puddle is the `puddle` sticker); no sea or beach (the lagoon is calm,
with no waves); no hill or slope near us (the meadow is flat; the
mountain and the cliffs are far away); no flowers painted on the meadow
(the magnolia is the `flower` sticker); no berries or fruit painted (the
`bush` sticker has them); no people; no smoke, fire or lava anywhere,
ever. Nothing changes the lagoon's size or the meadow's colour.

## What the canvas effects can and cannot change

Allowed (outdoors) and used here: `sunshine`, `clouds`, `wind`, `rain`,
`rainbow`, `fog`, `sunset`, `night`, `fireflies`, `shootingstars`,
`confetti`, `bubbles`. Not used: `snow` (a warm valley; the snow would
also cover the meadow), `leaves` (the tree fern and the monkey-puzzle
are evergreen: no autumn here).

- `sunshine`: warm light and soft shafts; the sunbathers' moment.
- `clouds`: big soft clouds drift over and the light dims a little; the
  painted clouds stay. Cloud shapes are the words' to tell.
- `wind`: blows left to right (from the lagoon towards the big rock), or
  right to left with `left`. Anything carried by it goes its way.
- `rain` / `rainbow`: rain over everything, then a rainbow behind the
  scenery. The lagoon and the meadow stay as painted: no new puddles,
  no flooding — the mud puddle sticker is the only mud.
- `fog`: a morning mist over the lagoon and the meadow; the far cliffs
  and the mountain fade but stay painted.
- `sunset` / `night`: golden-pink evening, then dark blue night with a
  moon. The mountain is just a dark shape: it does not glow.
- `fireflies`: tiny lights low over the meadow and the lagoon, over
  `sunset` or `night` only.
- `shootingstars`: over `night` only.
- `confetti`: a party, a hooray (a hatching, a birthday).
- `bubbles`: bubbles rising over the scene — the lagoon friends blowing
  them. Careful: the word "bubbles" / "burbujas" asks for this effect;
  a few bubbles from one sticker are `bubble-puff` and are better called
  "a bubble… and another" / "burbujitas".
